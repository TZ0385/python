# Challenge 01: A chart is packaged evidence

**Gate:** self-attested · **Points:** 10

Every chart is a claim about numbers. Before writing code, say out loud
what each of the three required charts *claims*:

- `revenue_by_region.png` claims: "region totals are X, Y, Z."
- `daily_revenue.png` claims: "the daily series looks like this."
- `revenue_histogram.png` claims: "row-level revenue is distributed
  like this."

If the claim is wrong, a beautiful chart is worse than no chart.

**Checkpoint:** you can state the claim behind each chart. Self-attested;
record via `python verify.py progress`.
